Set up your vault

Your vault lives only on this device. The server stores nothing — not your PIN, not your cards, not your balances. Everything is locked with your PIN (and an optional passphrase) before it is saved.

No recovery, by design. If you forget your PIN (and passphrase, if you set one), the vault cannot be opened by anyone — not even us. There is no "forgot PIN". Write your PIN down somewhere safe, or export an encrypted backup (Settings) after setup.

Unlock your vault

Enter your PIN to open the vault on this device.

5 wrong PINs lock the vault for a growing delay (30s, then doubling, up to 15 minutes). The vault locks itself after 5 minutes without use.

Second step — authenticator code

Two-factor is on. Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or any TOTP app) and enter the 6-digit code. You can also use one of your backup codes instead.

Wrong codes count toward the lockout, the same as wrong PINs.

Import a statement (CSV)

Download your statement CSV from your bank or wallet app, then upload it. Map the columns, review every row, approve the ones you want. Duplicates (same amount + date + merchant) are flagged and skipped.

Direction values: "debit"/"dr"/"withdrawal"/"debit" = money out, "credit"/"cr"/"deposit" = money in. If your CSV has no direction column, pick "— none (amount sign)" and negative amounts count as debit.

How your data is protected

Local-first: everything lives only in this browser, encrypted with PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM under your PIN. The server stores nothing. The vault never holds full card numbers, CVV, card PINs, expiry dates or bank passwords — only the last 4 digits, the provider, a nickname and the balance. A vault that cannot spend your money cannot be robbed.

Honest limit: software encryption is only as secure as this device. On a rooted phone, or a shared device left unlocked, someone with the app open can see the vault in plain text. Keep your device locked and your PIN private.

Honest limit: no live bank sync — no Pakistani bank or wallet offers a personal API today. Balances update when you add a transaction, approve a pasted SMS, or import a statement.

Two-factor: turn it on in Security settings and unlocking also needs a code from your authenticator app, plus 10 one-time backup codes for a lost phone. The secret lives inside the vault (protected by your PIN); the backup codes are stored as fingerprints only, so they cannot be shown again — generate a new set to replace them.
