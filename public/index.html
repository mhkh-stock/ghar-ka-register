Set up your home's book

Your book lives only on this device. The server stores nothing — not your PIN, not your entries. Everything is locked with your PIN (and an optional passphrase) before it is saved.

No recovery, by design. If you forget your PIN (and passphrase, if you set one), the book cannot be opened by anyone — not even us. There is no "forgot PIN". Write your PIN down somewhere safe, or export an encrypted backup (Backup & Import) after setup.

A fresh book starts empty. An old Card Vault backup does not open here — it restores only into the old code. Nothing is carried over.

Unlock your book

Enter your PIN to open the book on this device.

5 wrong PINs lock the book for a growing delay (30s, then doubling, up to 15 minutes). The book locks itself after 5 minutes without use.

How your book is protected

Local-first: everything lives only in this browser, encrypted with PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM under your PIN. The server stores nothing.

Honest limit: software encryption is only as secure as this device. On a shared device left unlocked, someone with the app open can see the book in plain text. Keep your device locked and your PIN private.
