Staff — helpers

Helpers, monthly salary, attendance, advances and month-end pay, worked out line by line. Every figure is as you entered it; every computed figure shows its working. First 2 absent days each month are free (paid leave) — or as many as the helper's paid leave allows. Extra absent days deduct one day's pay (salary ÷ 30); a half day deducts half that. Joined mid-month? Salary counts only from the join date.

How your book is protected

Local-first: everything lives only in this browser, encrypted with PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM under your PIN. The server stores nothing.

Honest limit: software encryption is only as secure as this device. On a shared device left unlocked, someone with the app open can see the book in plain text. Keep your device locked and your PIN private.
